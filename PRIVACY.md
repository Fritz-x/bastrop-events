# Bastrop Events: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what Bastrop Events (the "App"), a Reddit Developer Platform app operated
by u/neuralbladez (the "Developer"), does with information.

## Summary

The App collects no personal information. It does not read posts, comments, messages, votes, or
profiles, and it sends no Reddit data to anyone outside Reddit.

## Information the App handles

1. **Public event listings.** The App reads event titles, dates, times, venues, and categories
   from the two calendars listed below. These are public listings about events, not about
   Reddit users.
2. **One stored value per subreddit.** The App saves the date of the last week it posted, so it
   does not post the same week twice. This is kept in the storage Reddit provides to the App
   and contains no personal information.
3. **Install settings.** Moderators can set a post title, title patterns to leave out, and
   whether to pin the post. Reddit stores these with the installation.
4. **The moderator running a menu action.** When a moderator uses "post now", the App asks
   Reddit whether that account has permission to manage posts. The answer is used for that
   one check and is not stored.

## Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect, store, or share usernames, email addresses, IP addresses, location,
device details, browsing activity, or the content of anything users write on Reddit. It uses no
cookies, analytics, or advertising, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

## Outside services the App contacts

The App makes read only requests to two hosts to fetch public event listings:

* `bastropcountycalendar.com` (Bastrop County Calendar)
* `api.withapps.io` (host of the City of Bastrop community calendar, connect.cityofbastrop.org)

These requests contain no Reddit user data, no account identifiers, and no subreddit content.
As with any web request, those hosts can see that a request came from Reddit's servers.

## Where data lives and how long it is kept

Everything the App stores lives on Reddit's Developer Platform. The stored value and the
install settings remain until a moderator uninstalls the App, at which point Reddit removes the
App's data for that subreddit under its own platform practices. Posts the App has made stay on
Reddit until a moderator or Reddit removes them.

## Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
